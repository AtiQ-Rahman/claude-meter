# Privacy policy: Usage Meter for Claude

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Usage Meter for Claude does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension reads

- Your claude.ai organization list and usage limits, from claude.ai, using the login already in your browser. This is the same information claude.ai shows on Settings > Usage.
- Whether you pressed send on claude.ai, to count messages for the "messages left" estimate. It does not read what you write.

## Where data goes

- Requests go only to `https://claude.ai`.
- Readings, a 7-day usage history and your settings are stored in your browser (`chrome.storage`). Settings may sync between your own browsers through your browser account, like any extension setting.
- If you turn on "Send readings to the GNOME top bar", readings are passed to a program on your own computer that you installed. Nothing is sent over the network.

There are no analytics, tracking, ads or third-party servers.

## Removing data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
